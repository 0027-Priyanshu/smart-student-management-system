College Handbook & Rules

1. Attendance Policy:
- Minimum attendance required to appear for final exams is 75%.
- Students with attendance between 65% and 75% must submit a medical certificate.
- Attendance below 65% will result in automatic semester detention.

2. Grading System:
- A grade: 90-100% (GPA 4.0)
- B grade: 80-89% (GPA 3.0)
- C grade: 70-79% (GPA 2.0)
- D grade: 60-69% (GPA 1.0)
- F grade: Below 60% (Fail)

3. Leave Policy:
- Students can apply for 'On Leave' status up to 5 days per semester without penalty.
- Faculty must approve any leave beyond 5 days.

4. Library Rules:
- Books can be borrowed for a maximum of 14 days.
- A maximum of 5 books can be issued at a time.

5. Code of Conduct:
- Ragging is strictly prohibited and is a punishable offense.
- Use of mobile phones is restricted during lectures.
- Formal attire is expected during practical labs and presentations.
